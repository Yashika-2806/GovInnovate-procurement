## Mitigation Strategies
### PMF
- **Recommended Action:** Establish quantifiable health economics and outcome research (HEOR) early in pilots.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### COMPETITION
- **Recommended Action:** Focus on superior predictive accuracy, multi-source data ingestion, and explainable AI.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### BUSINESS_MODEL
- **Recommended Action:** Adopt an annual SaaS subscription model tiered by bed capacity or active patient volume.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### FINANCIAL
- **Recommended Action:** Maintain at least 24-36 months of cash runway during regulatory clearance phases.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### TECHNOLOGY
- **Recommended Action:** Build on standard HL7 FHIR protocols and provide continuous explainable AI score outputs.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### REGULATORY
- **Recommended Action:** Engage regulatory counsel early for Q-Submission meetings with the FDA.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### OPERATIONAL
- **Recommended Action:** Deploy dedicated clinical implementation managers for on-site training.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### TEAM
- **Recommended Action:** Recruit experienced healthcare executives and regulatory advisors.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score

### FUNDING
- **Recommended Action:** Target specialized healthtech VCs accustomed to FDA timelines and enterprise health cycles.
- **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in overall risk score
